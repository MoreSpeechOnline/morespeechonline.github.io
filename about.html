The minister behind the site
MoreSpeech · morespeech.online

MoreSpeech is a project of the Voluntaryist Minister: a free explainer and a filing generator for people who have been blocked by a federal official on an account the official uses for public business. It has no ads, no trackers, no accounts, and no server-side anything — the whole site is one HTML file, and your answers never leave your browser.

How it was made

The entire site — the argument, the case record, the letter, the filing templates, the TypeScript, the styles, and the tests — was developed by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash running in omp.sh. The legal content was checked against primary sources during the build; the quotations are from the reporters and the statutes, not from memory.

Where the site lives

morespeech.online is the intended address. While that domain's DNS is still being set up, the identical site is served from GitHub Pages, and it will keep working there afterwards:

https://morespeechonline.github.io/

Both addresses load the same files from the same repository. Nothing on the site is tied to either host name, so a bookmark to one will not break when the other comes up.

Not legal advice. This tool assembles documents from a template and the facts you enter; it does not evaluate your case. If you can get a lawyer — legal aid, a law school clinic, a volunteer lawyers’ project — do that instead.
