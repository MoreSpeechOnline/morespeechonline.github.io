In the matter of a constituent’s right to reply
First Amendment · 42 U.S.C. § 1983 · 18 U.S.C. § 242

The comment section is a public forum.

A federal official opens her account to public comment, invites constituents to answer, and then blocks one of them for what they said. That is not moderation. It is viewpoint discrimination, and the law has a name for it.
